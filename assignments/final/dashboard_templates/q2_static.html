The sinking of the Titanic is one of the most infamous shipwrecks in history. While there was some element of luck involved in surviving, some groups of people were more likely to survive than others.

This dashboard looks at who survived, using 712 passengers with a known age and port.

Data: titanic_final.csv

About this dashboard

This dashboard was built with Quarto. The Overview page shows the main numbers, Distributions compares age and fare for passengers who did and did not survive, and Data lets you search the passenger list.

Women and first-class passengers survived at much higher rates than men and third-class passengers.
